# Billing integrity: carve out detours longer than two minutes

FilOz is only billed for time actually spent on FilOz work. When drafting time entries, any detour longer than two minutes ends the FilOz time entry and a new one starts after it; gaps of five minutes or less with no activity are bridged; off-computer time is never billed unless the user adds it themselves. We chose this over Timing's own guidance (a handful of coarse entries per day that absorb short detours) because coarse entries would silently bill FilOz for personal and volunteering time.

## Consequences

Days produce many short time entries rather than a few long ones. This is expected, and the invoice pipeline handles it fine since it sums entries.
